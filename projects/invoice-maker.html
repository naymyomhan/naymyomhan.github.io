Features

Everything a small shop needs

A real POS on the phone or tablet you already own — no server, no monthly fee, and no internet needed at the counter.

  • Fast POS cashier

    Tap-to-add product grid, camera barcode scanning, live stock counts, and checkout as paid, credit or partial payment.

  • Invoice designer

    Five page layouts plus an 80 mm receipt roll, with your logo, shop details, colours, background, renamable labels and a draggable PAID stamp.

  • Print, share & save

    A5 printing through the system dialog, ESC/POS receipts to Bluetooth thermal printers, and invoice images shared to any chat app or saved to the gallery.

  • Items, stock & customers

    Product photos and barcodes, stock-in history, low-stock alerts, a customer directory, and CSV export of items and sales.

  • Credit book & daily closing

    Money to receive and to pay, expenses, 7-day insights, monthly profit, and a one-tap end-of-day report.

  • Backup & restore

    A one-file backup moves a whole shop — items, customers, invoices and debts — to a new phone in a single step.

Your shop data stays on your device

Products, stock, customers, invoices, debts and expenses are saved only in a database on your own phone or tablet. No accounts, no login, no cloud servers — we never receive, upload or sell your shop data.

About the project

Why I built it

Small shops, cafes and market stalls need a real POS, but most options require an account, a server, a monthly fee or a constant internet connection. Easy Invoice Maker & POS runs entirely on the phone or tablet a shop already owns.

My role

Creator and lead mobile developer. I built the cashier and checkout flow, on-device SQLite storage with backup & restore, pixel-identical invoice rendering at a fixed page width, ESC/POS raster printing for Bluetooth thermal printers, a tablet split-screen register, three-language localization, and Google AdMob monetization.

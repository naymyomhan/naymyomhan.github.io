QUEST #04 ● ANDROID APP MOBILE POS & INVOICING

EASY INVOICE MAKER & POS

A 100% offline point-of-sale and invoice maker for small shops. Ring up sales in seconds, print Bluetooth thermal receipts, design branded invoices with a movable PAID stamp, track customer debts, and close the day with one-tap reports — in English, Myanmar and Thai.

FEED: EASY INVOICE MAKER & POS // OFFICIAL FEATURE GRAPHIC

📱 MOBILE APPLICATION INTERFACE

01 // POS CASHIER & BARCODE
02 // BRANDED INVOICE & PAID STAMP
03 // DAILY OVERVIEW & ALERTS

📜 QUEST BRIEF & DEV ROLE

Mission: Give small shops, cafes and market stalls a real POS on the phone or tablet they already own — no account, no server, no monthly fee, and no internet needed at the counter.

Engineering Role: Creator & Lead Mobile Developer (A Dev Dude / Nay Myo Mhan). Built the cashier and checkout flow, on-device SQLite storage with backup & restore, pixel-identical invoice rendering at a fixed page width, ESC/POS raster printing for Bluetooth thermal printers, a tablet split-screen register, three-language localization, and Google AdMob monetization.

[ EQUIPPED TECH STACK ]
💙 Flutter & Dart 🧠 GetX 🗄️ SQLite 🖨️ ESC/POS Bluetooth 📄 PDF Printing 📷 Barcode Scanner 📢 Google AdMob

⚡ CORE MODULES

  • 🛒
    Fast POS Cashier: Tap-to-add product grid, camera barcode scanning, live stock counts, and checkout as paid, credit or partial payment.
  • 🧾
    Invoice Designer: Five page layouts plus an 80mm receipt roll, with your logo, shop details, colours, background, renamable labels and a draggable PAID stamp.
  • 🖨️
    Print, Share & Save: A5 printing through the system dialog, ESC/POS receipts to Bluetooth thermal printers, and invoice images shared to any chat app or saved to the gallery.
  • 📦
    Items, Stock & Customers: Product photos and barcodes, stock-in history, low-stock alerts, a customer directory, and CSV export of items and sales.
  • 💰
    Credit Book & Daily Closing: Money to receive and to pay, expenses, 7-day insights, monthly profit, and a one-tap end-of-day report.
  • 🔒
    100% On-Device Data: Everything stays in a local database; one-file JSON backup & restore moves a whole shop to a new phone.

Core modules

From raw footage to publish-ready assets

An integrated AI workspace that replaces a stack of separate post-production tools.

  • AI video recap

    Condenses long videos into accurate chapter breakdowns, key insights and timestamps.

  • Neural voice & cloning

    Multi-speaker AI narration with expressive tone synthesis and custom voice cloning.

  • Auto subtitles & SRT

    Fast multilingual speech-to-text alignment with synchronized Myanmar and English subtitle downloads.

  • Viral clips extractor

    Detects engaging segments and auto-frames vertical shorts for TikTok and Reels.

  • Fast cloud pipeline

    Streaming processing architecture for low-latency rendering and high availability.

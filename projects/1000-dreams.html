Features

Find a dream and its numbers in seconds

The classic Myanmar dream books, brought together in one clean app with no account, no sign-in and no internet needed for the content.

  • 1000 illustrated dreams

    Every dream with its drawing, three-digit number, and Myanmar and English name. Narrow the book down by first digit (0–9) in one tap.

  • Round & up-down numbers

    Each dream links to the dreams for every reordering of its digits and for the numbers directly above and below it.

  • 100 dreams & 100 lucky animals

    Picture cards with two-digit numbers and Myanmar, English and Thai names, plus a zoomable full-screen picture view.

  • Dream dictionary

    2,600+ readings grouped by Myanmar letter, with full-text search and highlighted matches.

  • Search everything

    One search box across all four books. Type a number in Myanmar digits (၅၄) or Latin digits (54), or any word.

  • Light & dark themes

    A compact, monochrome interface that is easy on the eyes day or night.

Private & offline

All dream content — pictures, numbers and dictionary readings — ships inside the app and works offline. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the app does not collect personal information. Searches never leave your device.

About the project

Why I built it

To bring the classic Myanmar dream books to the phone in one clean, compact app — so anyone can find what a dream means and its lucky numbers in seconds, without an account, a sign-in or an internet connection.

My role

Creator and lead mobile developer. I built the Flutter app, bundled and parsed the dream data on a background isolate, designed the compact monochrome light & dark interface, wrote the search with Myanmar-digit support, and integrated Google AdMob with frequency-capped placements and Google’s consent (UMP) flow.

Dream readings and numbers are traditional folklore, provided for cultural reference and entertainment only.

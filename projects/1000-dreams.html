QUEST #03 ● NEW APP MOBILE DREAM BOOK

1000 DREAMS: MYANMAR DREAM BOOK

အိပ်မက် ဟောကိန်း — a fast, fully offline Myanmar dream book for Android. Look up the 1000 illustrated dreams with their lucky numbers, the 100 dreams and 100 lucky animals, and a dream dictionary of 2,600+ readings, with one search that understands Myanmar words, English names and Myanmar digits.

FEED: 1000 DREAMS // OFFICIAL BANNER & ARTWORK

📱 MOBILE APPLICATION INTERFACE

01 // HOME & DREAM OF THE DAY
02 // 1000 ILLUSTRATED DREAMS
03 // ROUND & UP-DOWN NUMBERS
04 // 100 LUCKY ANIMALS
05 // DREAM DICTIONARY SEARCH
06 // LIGHT & DARK THEMES

📜 QUEST BRIEF & DEV ROLE

Mission: Bring the classic Myanmar dream books to the phone in one clean, compact app. Find what a dream means and its lucky numbers in seconds, with no account, no sign-in and no internet needed for the content.

Engineering Role: Creator & Lead Mobile Developer (A Dev Dude / Nay Myo Mhan). Built the Flutter app, bundled and parsed the dream data on a background isolate, designed the compact monochrome light & dark interface, wrote the search with Myanmar-digit support, and integrated Google AdMob with frequency-capped placements and Google's consent (UMP) flow.

Note: Dream readings and numbers are traditional folklore, provided for cultural reference and entertainment only.

[ EQUIPPED TECH STACK ]
💙 Flutter 🎯 Dart 📱 Android 📦 Offline Data 🌗 Light & Dark Themes 📢 Google AdMob 🔒 UMP Consent

⚡ CORE MODULES

  • 📖
    1000 Illustrated Dreams: Every dream with its drawing, three-digit number, Myanmar and English name. Narrow the book down by first digit (0–9) in one tap.
  • 🔁
    Round & Up-Down Numbers: Each dream links to the dreams for every reordering of its digits and for the numbers directly above and below it.
  • 🐉
    100 Dreams & 100 Lucky Animals: Picture cards with two-digit numbers and Myanmar, English and Thai names, plus a zoomable full-screen picture view.
  • 🔤
    Dream Dictionary: 2,600+ readings grouped by Myanmar letter, with full-text search and highlighted matches.
  • 🔍
    Search Everything: One search box across all four books. Type a number in Myanmar digits (၅၄) or Latin digits (54), or any word.
  • 🔒
    Private & Offline: All content ships inside the app. No account, no sign-in, and no personal data collected by the app.

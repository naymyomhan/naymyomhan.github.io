Features

Professional tools without the desktop complexity

Multi-layer painting and timeline animation for game developers, artists and retro fans — on the device you already carry.

  • 16-layer canvas

    Up to 16 independent layers with opacity blending, canvas presets from 16×16 up to 256×256 plus custom sizes, and curated retro palettes.

  • Frame-by-frame animation

    A multi-frame timeline with onion skinning, frame duplication and real-time playback for smooth game sprites.

  • Photo to pixel art

    Fast colour quantization turns camera photos and gallery pictures into stylized pixel art sprites.

  • Phone & tablet layouts

    A compact one-hand layout on phones and a pro tablet studio with a tool rail, docked palette & layers and a bigger canvas, in portrait or landscape.

  • GIF & sprite-sheet export

    Export animated GIFs, sprite sheets for Unity and Godot, high-resolution PNGs, and project files.

  • Select, transform & recolor

    Select areas to move and transform them, and recolor artwork with live hue, saturation and lightness controls.

Your artwork stays on your device

Drawings, animation frames, layers and custom palettes are saved locally. There are no cloud servers — your art leaves the device only when you export or share it yourself.

About the project

Why I built it

To give game developers, artists and retro fans a smooth, frictionless pixel art tool on mobile — removing the complexity of desktop software while keeping professional multi-layer painting and timeline animation.

My role

Creator and lead mobile developer. I engineered the raster drawing engine, the multi-frame timeline with real-time onion skinning, colour quantization for photo conversion, palette management, the adaptive tablet layout, and Google AdMob monetization.

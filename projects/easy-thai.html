A free, fully offline Android app that teaches Thai to Myanmar speakers living and working in Thailand. Learn the whole Thai alphabet with its sounds, work through 26 short lessons, and find the phrase you need at work, at the hospital or at the market — every word with its pronunciation written in Burmese.

Features

Everything you need to start speaking Thai

Five tabs — Alphabet, Lessons, Phrases, Grammar and Dictionary — with no account, no sign-in and no internet needed to learn.

  • The whole Thai alphabet

    All 44 consonants colour-coded by class, 31 vowels, 4 tone marks and 10 numerals — each with its sound, the closest Burmese letter and a word to remember it by.

  • 26 short lessons

    Three levels and nine units, from the alphabet to conversations at work and at the market, read one card at a time. A practice deck lets you test yourself — no scores, nothing recorded.

  • 149 phrases for real life

    Thirteen situations — emergencies, the workplace, the hospital, police and immigration, the bank, renting a room — with male and female polite forms.

  • “Show” mode

    One tap fills the screen with the Thai text so you can turn your phone around and let a Thai speaker read it — for noisy places, or when you are unsure of your pronunciation.

  • Dictionary & grammar

    630 words you can search in Thai, Burmese or English, and 13 grammar notes written in Burmese. Tap any Thai word in a note to hear it; hold it to hear it slowly.

  • Workers’ rights & help

    Wages and overtime, social security, the 90-day report and your documents, explained in Burmese — with tap-to-call numbers for the police, ambulance and labour hotlines.

Private & offline

Every lesson, word and phrase ships inside the app and works without internet. There are no accounts and no progress tracking; your bookmarks and settings stay on your phone. Only the ads use the network.

About the project

Why I built it

Many people from Myanmar work in Thailand and need Thai every day — at work, at the hospital, at the market and with their paperwork — but most Thai courses are written for English speakers. Easy Thai teaches Thai in Burmese, on any phone, without an account or an internet connection.

My role

Creator and lead mobile developer. I built the Flutter app, bundled all the content as offline data, wrote Burmese-script pronunciations for every word and phrase, made the speaker buttons find a Thai voice on whichever text-to-speech engine the phone has, bundled a Burmese font so older Android phones show the text correctly, and integrated Google AdMob with frequency-capped placements and no ads on the emergency, rights or “show” screens.

The workers’ rights pages were checked in September 2026 and are general information, not legal advice. Laws change — confirm with the 1546 labour hotline before acting on them.

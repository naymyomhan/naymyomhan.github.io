## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal Data & Artwork
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise geolocation.
- **100% Local Storage:** All your creative works—including pixel art drawings, sprite sheets, animation frames, custom palettes, and layer configurations—are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not run cloud backup servers or upload your artwork without your explicit manual export. Your creations belong solely to you.

### B. Device Permissions
The app may request access to certain features of your device only when required:
- **Photos / Media / Storage Access:** Required when you choose to import an existing photo to convert into pixel art ("From photo"), or when you save or share your finished exports (PNG, JPEG, GIF, Sprite Sheets). The app does not access, read, or upload any other media on your device.
- **Internet & Network State Access:** Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load and display advertisements.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pixel Art Maker is an all-ages creative tool designed for hobbyists, artists, and game developers. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information through third-party services, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Pixel Art Maker operates primarily offline with local storage:
- You have complete control over all data created inside the app.
- You can delete any individual project, frame, layer, or exported file directly inside the application.
- If you uninstall the app or clear the app's cache and data through your device settings, all locally stored artwork and app settings will be permanently erased.

---

## 6. Security

We value your trust in using Pixel Art Maker. Since your artwork is saved locally on your device, standard operating system sandbox security protects your data from unauthorized access by other applications.

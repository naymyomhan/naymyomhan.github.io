Privacy Policy

Easy Invoice Maker & POS · Android app by A Dev Dude

  • Effective date: November 17, 2026
  • Last updated: November 17, 2026
  • Developer: A Dev Dude

Easy Invoice Maker & POS (“we”, “our”, or “the app”, Android package com.adevdude.invoicemakerpos) is developed by A Dev Dude as an ad-supported mobile application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and which third parties are involved.

Privacy guarantee: Your products, stock, customers, invoices, debts and expenses are saved only in a database on your own device. The app has no accounts, no login and no cloud servers. We never receive, upload, or sell your shop data.

1. Information collection and use

A. Your shop data

  • Stored on your device only: Everything you enter — shop name, address and logo, items and photos, stock levels, customer names, phone numbers and addresses, invoices, credit/debt records and expenses — is stored in a local database inside the app’s private storage. It is never sent to us.
  • Customer details you enter: If you record a customer’s name, phone number or address, that information stays on your device and is used only to print it on invoices and to track their balance in your credit book. You are responsible for having permission to store your customers’ details.
  • No personal data collected by us: We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or any other personally identifiable information.

B. Device permissions

The app asks for device features only when you use the matching function:

  • Camera: Used only to scan product barcodes while you are on the scanner screen. Camera frames are processed on the device and are never saved or uploaded.
  • Bluetooth (Nearby devices): Used only to list your paired Bluetooth thermal printers and send receipts to the printer you choose. The app does not use Bluetooth to determine your location.
  • Photos: When you add a shop logo, an invoice background or a product photo, the system photo picker lets you choose a single image. The app sees only the image you pick.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only): Used to save invoice images to your gallery when you tap “Save to gallery”. Newer Android versions need no storage permission for this.
  • Internet & Network State: Required only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load advertisements. The app itself works fully offline.

C. Backups, exports and sharing

  • Backups: “Back up” writes your whole shop database to a single JSON file on your device and opens your phone’s share sheet. Where that file goes (Telegram, Google Drive, email, etc.) is entirely your choice; we never receive a copy.
  • Invoices, receipts & reports: Images, PDFs, CSV exports and daily closing reports are created on your device and leave it only when you share, save or print them yourself.

2. Third-party service providers and advertising

To keep the app free, Easy Invoice Maker & POS shows native, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad can unlock premium invoice layouts or Bluetooth thermal printing for 24 hours.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information in accordance with its own privacy policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to estimate general, coarse location such as country or city)
  • Operating system version and device model
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

AdMob never receives your shop data, customers, invoices or other records stored in the app.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google’s official privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites
  • Google Advertising Privacy Policy

How to opt out of personalized ads

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads anytime directly through your device settings: Android Settings → Google → Ads → tap “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.

3. Log data and crash reporting

The app does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service. If the app crashes, Google Play may share anonymous diagnostic reports with us through the Google Play Console to help fix bugs. These reports do not contain your shop data, customers or invoices.

4. Children’s privacy

Easy Invoice Maker & POS is a business tool intended for shop owners and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because all shop data lives on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • You can edit or delete any item, customer, invoice, debt or expense inside the app.
  • Settings → Erase everything permanently deletes all shop data from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings also permanently erases everything stored by the app. Keep a backup file if you want to restore your shop later.

6. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by the Android operating system sandbox from other apps. Backup files you export are ordinary files, so store and share them only with people and services you trust.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

Privacy Policy

1000 Dreams (အိပ်မက် ဟောကိန်း) · Android app by A Dev Dude

  • Effective date: November 17, 2026
  • Last updated: November 18, 2026
  • Developer: A Dev Dude

1000 Dreams — အိပ်မက် ဟောကိန်း (“we”, “our”, or “the app”, Android package com.adevdude.dreamapp) is developed by A Dev Dude as an ad-supported mobile application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to use our Service.

Privacy guarantee: All dream content — pictures, numbers and dictionary readings — is bundled inside the app and works offline. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information.

1. Information collection and use

A. Personal data

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • No accounts: The app does not require or offer registration or sign-in.
  • Searches stay on your device: Anything you type into the app’s search boxes is matched against the content bundled in the app and is never sent to us or to any server.
  • Language choice: The language you pick (English, Myanmar or Thai) is saved only on your device, so the app opens in it next time. It is never sent to us.

B. Device permissions

The app asks for as little as possible:

  • Internet & Network State Access: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load and display advertisements. The dream content itself never needs the internet.
  • Advertising ID: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may read your device’s Google Advertising ID to serve and measure ads (see Section 2).
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files.

2. Third-party service providers and advertising

To keep the app completely free, 1000 Dreams shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial and app open ads).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information in accordance with its own privacy policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to determine general coarse location, e.g., country/city level)
  • Operating system version and device hardware model
  • In-app ad interaction and diagnostic metrics

Ad consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. In those regions you can review or change your choice at any time with the ad privacy settings link at the bottom of the app’s home screen.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google’s official privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites
  • Google Advertising Privacy Policy

How to opt out of personalized ads

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads anytime directly through your device settings: Android Settings → Google → Ads → tap “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.

3. Log data and crash reporting

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK of its own. In the event of an unexpected error or crash, standard diagnostic reports may be processed through the Google Play Console to help us find and fix bugs. These automated reports do not contain personal user data.

4. Children’s privacy (COPPA compliance)

1000 Dreams is a general-audience reference app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores no personal data:

  • There is no account or profile for us to keep or delete.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, removes everything the app has stored on your device.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies; you can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time as described in Section 2.

6. Security

We value your trust in using 1000 Dreams. The app keeps no personal data, and the content it ships with is protected by the standard Android application sandbox.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, features, or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this webpage with an updated “Effective Date”.

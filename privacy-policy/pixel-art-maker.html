PRIVACY POLICY: PIXEL ART MAKER

EFFECTIVE DATE: SEPTEMBER 17, 2026 • LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 17, 2026 • DEVELOPER: A DEV DUDE • APPLICATION: PIXEL ART MAKER

Pixel Art Maker (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is developed by A Dev Dude (Nay Myo Mhan) as an ad-supported mobile application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to use our Service.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee: All pixel drawings, animation frames, layers, and custom color palettes are saved 100% locally on your device. We do not operate cloud storage servers, and we never upload, store, or sell your creative artwork.

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE

[ A. PERSONAL DATA & ARTWORK ]

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise geolocation.
  • 100% Local Storage: All creative works—including pixel art drawings, sprite sheets, animation frames, custom palettes, and layer configurations—are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not upload your artwork without your explicit manual export. Your creations belong solely to you.

[ B. DEVICE PERMISSIONS ]

The app requests access to device features strictly when necessary for creative functionality:

  • Photos / Media / Storage Access: Required when you choose to import an existing photo to convert into pixel art (“From photo”), or when you save or share your finished exports (PNG, JPEG, GIF, Sprite Sheets). The app never reads or accesses any other files on your device.
  • Internet & Network State Access: Required exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load and display banner/interstitial advertisements.

2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS & ADVERTISING

To support the ongoing development of the app and keep it completely free for all users, Pixel Art Maker integrates third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information in accordance with its privacy policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to determine general coarse location, e.g., country/city level)
  • Operating system version and device hardware model
  • In-app ad interaction and diagnostic metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google's official privacy policies:

  • 👉 Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites
  • 👉 Google Advertising Privacy Policy
⚙️ How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads:
You can manage or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads anytime directly through your device settings:
Android Settings → Google → Ads → Tap “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.

3. LOG DATA & CRASH REPORTING

In the event of an unexpected application error or crash, standard diagnostic logs and device metrics may be processed via the Google Play Developer Console to help us identify and resolve bugs. These automated reports do not contain personal user data, passwords, or artwork content.

4. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

Pixel Art Maker is an all-ages creative tool designed for hobbyists, artists, and game developers. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Because Pixel Art Maker operates primarily offline with local storage:

  • You retain total ownership and control over all data created inside the app.
  • You can delete any individual project, frame, layer, or exported file directly inside the application interface.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear the app's cache and data through device settings, all locally stored artwork and settings will be permanently erased.

6. SECURITY

We value your trust in using Pixel Art Maker. Because all your artwork is saved locally on your device, standard operating system sandbox security protects your data from unauthorized access by other applications.

7. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, features, or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly within the app and on this webpage with an updated “Effective Date”.

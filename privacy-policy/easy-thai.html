Privacy Policy

Easy Thai (ထိုင်းစကား လွယ်လွယ်ကူကူ) · Android app by A Dev Dude

  • Effective date: November 19, 2026
  • Last updated: November 19, 2026
  • Developer: A Dev Dude

Easy Thai (“we”, “our”, or “the app”, Android package com.easythai.adevdude) is developed by A Dev Dude as an ad-supported mobile application that teaches Thai to Myanmar speakers. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to use our Service.

Privacy guarantee: All lessons, words, phrases and grammar notes are bundled inside the app and work offline. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no progress tracking, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information.

1. Information collection and use

A. Personal data

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • No accounts and no tracking: The app does not require or offer registration or sign-in, and it does not record which lessons you open, your practice answers, or any scores.
  • Searches stay on your device: Anything you type into the dictionary or phrasebook search is matched against the content bundled in the app and is never sent to us or to any server.
  • Saved on your device only: Your bookmarks (saved words, phrases and letters) and your settings — interface language (Myanmar or English), speech speed, theme, text size and whether pronunciations are shown — are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us.

B. Text-to-speech

When you tap a speaker button, the Thai text is read aloud by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example Google Text-to-Speech or your phone maker’s engine). Easy Thai passes the text only to that engine on your device; how the engine itself handles it is described in its own privacy policy.

C. Phone numbers and links

  • Tapping a help or emergency number in the rights pages opens your phone’s dialer with the number filled in. The app cannot place calls itself and cannot see your call history.
  • The settings screen can open your phone’s settings or Google Play to help you install a Thai voice. Nothing about you is sent when it does.

D. Device permissions

The app asks for as little as possible:

  • Internet & Network State Access: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load and display advertisements. The learning content never needs the internet.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may read your device’s Google Advertising ID and use Android’s ad services to serve and measure ads (see Section 2). The SDK may also briefly keep the device awake or run background work to finish ad-related tasks.
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, or the ability to make phone calls.

2. Third-party service providers and advertising

To keep the app completely free, Easy Thai shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). No ads are shown on the workers’ rights pages, the emergency phrases, or the full-screen “show” mode.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information in accordance with its own privacy policies, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to determine general coarse location, e.g., country/city level)
  • Operating system version and device hardware model
  • In-app ad interaction and diagnostic metrics

Ad consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform when it starts, before ads are personalized. To make a new choice later, clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Easy Thai → Storage → Clear data) and open the app again — note that this also removes your bookmarks and settings — or contact us.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please consult Google’s official privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites
  • Google Advertising Privacy Policy

How to opt out of personalized ads

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads anytime directly through your device settings: Android Settings → Google → Ads → tap “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID”.

3. Log data and crash reporting

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK of its own. In the event of an unexpected error or crash, standard diagnostic reports may be processed through the Google Play Console to help us find and fix bugs. These automated reports do not contain personal user data.

4. Children’s privacy (COPPA compliance)

Easy Thai is a general-audience learning app for adults and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Workers’ rights information

The workers’ rights pages (wages, social security, the 90-day report, documents and help numbers) are general information, last checked in September 2026. They are not legal advice and are not provided by, or affiliated with, any government agency. Laws and phone numbers can change, so please confirm with the official source (for example the 1546 labour hotline) before acting on them.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores no personal data:

  • There is no account or profile for us to keep or delete.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, removes everything the app has stored on your device, including bookmarks and settings.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies; you can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time as described in Section 2.

7. Security

We value your trust in using Easy Thai. The app keeps no personal data, and the content it ships with is protected by the standard Android application sandbox.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, features, or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this webpage with an updated “Effective Date”.
